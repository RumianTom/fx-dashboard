💰 Pricing Model

Every transaction follows this flow:

  1. Base price is set in EUR (e.g. a transfer costs €300)
  2. Customer pays in USD → we convert using the FX rate + our 3% markup
  3. The full customer payment is our GMV (the base price revenue goes to us)
  4. Provider cost is only the 1% FX conversion fee they charge us
  5. Our FX margin = markup revenue (3%) − provider fee (1%) = 2% target
StepCalculationAmount
Base price—€300.00
Customer pays (GMV)€300 × 1.18 × 1.03$364.62
FX markup revenue€300 × 1.18 × 0.03$10.62
Provider FX fee€300 × 1.18 × 0.01$3.54
Our FX margin$10.62 − $3.54$7.08 (1.94%)

The risk from holding a fixed FX rate is that the real rate moves — if EUR strengthens, our fixed rate was too low and the provider charges us more for the conversion.

⏱️ What Is a Fixation Window?

The FX provider gives us a new EUR/USD rate every 15 seconds. We fix (hold) the rate for a longer period to show customers consistent pricing.

WindowMeaning
15 secondsUse the provider's rate as-is (baseline)
1 hourRefresh our displayed rate every hour
12 hoursRefresh twice a day
24 hoursOne fixed rate per day
